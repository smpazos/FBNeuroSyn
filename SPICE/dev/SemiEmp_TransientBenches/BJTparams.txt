**** Area adjustment fot total bipolar current within the model
.param AreaPar 0.7e-5
**** Bipolar model parameters for direct mode. these set the bipolar current window between off and on
.param IsPar 1e-12
.param BfPar 200
.param VafPar 40
**** rePar linerizes the bipolar output current in the fired state (fit to the final section (high VD) of the Id-Vd characteristic under firing conditions.
.param rePar 0.1
.param CjPar 1e-15

**** Exponential factor for nonlinear junction capacitances (voltage dependance). No effect in DC
.param MjPar 1
.param NfPar 1.3

**** NePar directly affects avalanche relaxation time/voltage (i.e., when the hysteresis happens during the backward) Insensitive for NePar > 5****
.param NePar 5

**** VarPar also affects the quickness (slope) of the avalanche relaxation (insensitive for VarPar > 10)
.param VarPar 1.4

**** nbvPar affects the voltage of the avalanche relaxation (keep below 5 for ease of convergence)
***500 nm
*.param nbvPar '9-(0.1/Vg)*5.5'
***180 nm
*.param nbvPar '9-(0.4/Vg)*0.5'
.param nbvPar 3.4

**** BVPar is avalanche model onset voltage. Dependence is slight with VG, and needs to be larger than VD max within the simulation for avoiding convergence issues.
***500 nm
*.param BVPar '4-(1.5*Vg)'
***180 nm
.param BVPar '1.6+(0.6/Vg)'
*.param BVPar 2.5
